## What Mechanize Does — And How AUTOMECHANICA Leapfrogs It

### What AI Labs Admire in Mechanize:
Mechanize pioneered high-speed, deterministic developer sandboxes that generate high-quality reinforcement learning reward signals for coding agents. They proved that testing models inside realistic, interactive environments produces vastly superior models compared to static benchmarks.

### How AUTOMECHANICA Out-Innovates:
We took that foundation and pushed it to the ultimate frontier: **Embodied 3D Spatial Reasoning and Generative World Simulation**.

1. **VOXEL-GYM (3D Multi-Agent Open World & Minecraft Sandbox)**:
   - A real-time 3D Voxel Open World running at 60 FPS WebGL (Three.js) with procedural terrain, day/night cycles, and block physics.
   - **4 Autonomous AI Avatars** (Claude 3.7, GPT-4.5, DeepSeek R1, Gemini 3 Pro) running wild, pathfinding over 3D terrain, mining resources, and competing in real-time to erect colossal architectural megastructures (*Emerald Cyber-Spire*, *Quantum Monolith Pyramid*, *Obsidian Fortress*, *Solar Hyper-Gate*).
   - **First-Person Minecraft Player Mode**: Users can jump into the 3D world, walk around, mine, and build alongside the AI avatars.
   - **Architectus Spatial Reasoning Judge**: Real-time evaluation of verticality, structural symmetry, palette harmony, and construction velocity.

2. **The 5-Genre GameDev Matrix**:
   - 5 fully playable, AI-generated games: *Cyber-Pac Protocol*, *Neural Asteroids (Newtonian 2D Physics)*, *Cyber-Racer (2.5D Raycasting)*, *Quantum Tower Defense*, and *Matrix Roguelike (Procedural BSP Dungeon)*.

3. **Model Battle Royale Arena**:
   - Live split-screen head-to-head racing between frontier models on quantum gate transpilation, memory leak patching, and zero-day exploit defense.

4. **MCTS Trajectory Tree Inspector & SFT/DPO Exporter**:
   - Visual Monte Carlo Tree Search explorer showing $Q(s, a)$ rollout values and pruned branches with one-click OpenAI/Anthropic/DPO JSONL export.
